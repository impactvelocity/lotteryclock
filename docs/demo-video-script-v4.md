What if you couldn't trust your alarm clock? What if you never knew what time it really was? Are you late, or can you snooze an extra five minutes?

Introducing LotteryClock: the alarm clock that turns your mornings into a lottery of punctuality.

It was built for Kiroween using the Kiro IDE.

The idea is a resurrection. Dylan has had it since he was a kid, when he accidentally unplugged his alarm clock and couldn't tell how long it had been off. Turns out, not knowing the real time was the best alarm he'd ever had.

The way it works is simple. Set the time you want to wake up, say 7:30, then dial up your crazy. If you're just playing, add 5 minutes. If you're full-on Looney Tunes, set 45.

LotteryClock then sets the alarm for a random time inside that range. You could win a snooze, or lose and be up to 45 minutes late.

If you win, the snooze is set automatically to the time you won. That could be an extra 3 minutes, or 38 minutes of pure bliss.
