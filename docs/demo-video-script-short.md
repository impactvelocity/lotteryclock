Gather round. I've got a scary story.

Dylan was always late for school. He didn't oversleep. He snoozed. Professionally.

One day he cleaned his room, which is honestly the scariest part of this story, and tripped over the alarm clock's cord. He plugged it back in like nothing happened.

The next morning it went off, and his hand stopped halfway to snooze. Is that the real time? How long was it unplugged?

He had no idea. So he got up. He was on time for days, until he fixed the clock.

In 2012 he tried to turn that clock into an iPhone app. It never shipped. Fourteen years later, Dylan dug it back up for Kiroween's Resurrection category.

This is LotteryClock. Say you need to be up at 7:30. Drag the dial to fifteen minutes and tap Set Alarm.

Your iPhone now has a real alarm, set for a random minute between 7:15 and 7:45. The phone knows which minute. You don't.

It rings at 7:21. "Snooze until 7:30." You won nine minutes.

It rings at 7:38. "No snooze. Up you get." You're eight minutes late.

Either way, you have to open your eyes and check. And once your eyes are open, you're most of the way out of bed.

Count sheep, ask the spirits, or rise from the dead. Every morning goes into your history, so you can count exactly how many minutes of sleep you've gambled away.
